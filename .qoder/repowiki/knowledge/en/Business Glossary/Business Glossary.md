### MDCAT
- Definition：Medical and Dental College Admission Test — the Pakistani national entrance exam that MedAce AI prepares students for; the MVP targets a single subject (Biology) within this exam before expanding to other subjects.
- Aliases：MDCAT exam

### weak areas
- Definition：Per-student topic-level mistake tracking that drives adaptive quiz sessions and weekly study plans; the system re-drills topics where a student repeatedly answers incorrectly rather than serving random questions.
- Aliases：weak-topic memory、mistake tracking

### adaptive quiz
- Definition：A quiz session that prioritizes weak-area topics based on each student's historical mistakes, instead of drawing uniformly from the syllabus tree.
- Aliases：adaptive quiz sessions

### weekly study plan
- Definition：An AI-generated, per-student schedule produced by Gemini that tells a student what to study next and adapts as their weak areas improve over time.
- Aliases：study plan

### MVP wedge
- Definition：The go-to-market strategy of launching with exactly one exam (MDCAT) and one subject (Biology), done thoroughly, before adding more subjects or exams.
- Aliases：wedge
